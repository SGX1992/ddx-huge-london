The conversation

Where design
and innovation
go next.

Every leader in the room is being asked the same thing right now: what does your function look like when the tools can do a first draft of almost anything?

That question is too big for a panel and too interesting for a keynote. So we are putting a small group around one table instead — people running design, product, brand and innovation inside companies where the answer actually has consequences — and letting the conversation go where it goes. Huge brings the practitioners who are rebuilding how work gets made. DDX brings the room.

Your evening

The conversation
happens over dinner.

An unhurried evening in central London. You arrive, you are introduced to everyone, you sit down, and then one question opens the table. Nobody presents.

A proper dinner

A private room, a long table and a menu worth sitting at. Drinks on arrival, introductions before anyone sits down, so no one spends the night guessing who is who.

One question, opened

Huge and DDX open the table with a single question about the next twelve months, then get out of the way. Everything after that belongs to the room.

Small on purpose

Fifteen people at one table, so everyone can be heard. Leaders in brand, innovation and design — curated by DDX, hosted by Huge.
